Describing a Shell

On the table sat a small shell. The shell had many interesting features. One feature was its curved outline, which looked like a spiral.

The students touched the shell gently. They noticed the texture right away. Parts of the shell felt smooth, but other parts felt bumpy.

The surface of the shell had light and dark lines. The lines went around the shell and helped show its shape. The students carefully described what they saw and felt.

By observing the shell closely, the students learned how details help describe an object clearly.
